## Address and HTTPS

The portfolio is live on the clean, zero-budget GitHub Pages fallback and is served over HTTPS. A paid personal domain is intentionally deferred rather than purchased without the owner's approval.

## Analytics

The root includes a privacy-friendly anonymous pageview counter using Counter API's public, authentication-free endpoint. It records a cumulative pageview only; the portfolio does not send form values, names, email addresses, client identifiers, or private project data.

The counter is non-blocking. If the service is unavailable or rate-limited, the portfolio remains fully usable.

## Launch hygiene

- Unique page title and description.
- Canonical public URL.
- Open Graph title, description, URL, and image.
- SVG favicon and browser theme color.
- Responsive layout checked at phone, tablet, and desktop widths.
- Public paper, notebook, GitHub, agent, and evidence links.
